ℹ️ How this model works & tips ▶

Type or slide: Click any value box to type an exact number (with or without commas), or drag the slider. Percentage inputs automatically show their dollar equivalents.

Hover over tiles & waterfall rows: Hover over any multi-variable tile or waterfall line (marked with ∑) to see the exact formula and numbers behind it.

Apples-to-apples Net Worth methodology: Both the Buyer and Renter start with the same upfront cash and spend the same monthly cash flow. Any upfront cash or monthly savings the Renter doesn't spend on housing is invested in the stock market (minus a 1-month security deposit locked at 0% return). Conversely, in years where owning is cheaper than renting, the Buyer invests the monthly savings.

Today's Dollars toggle (top bar): Expresses all future net worth and cost figures in today's purchasing power, discounted by the inflation rate slider.
