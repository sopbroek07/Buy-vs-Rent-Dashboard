Apples-to-apples Net Worth methodology: Both Buyer and Renter start with the same upfront cash and spend the same monthly cash envelope. Any upfront cash or monthly savings the Renter doesn't spend on housing is invested in stocks (minus a 1-month security deposit locked at 0% return). Conversely, in years owning costs less than renting, the Buyer invests the monthly savings.
